Researcher Access Grant

Helping African early-career health researchers get their work into the room.

Important health research is being conducted across Africa, but having an abstract accepted at a scientific meeting does not always mean a researcher has the resources to attend. The MHH Researcher Access Grant is a small pilot designed to help close that last-mile funding gap.

At a glance

  • Award: one grant of up to €1,000.
  • Purpose: contribute to eligible costs associated with presenting accepted research at a recognised scientific conference or congress.
  • Fields: health, medicine, public health, health systems and closely related fields.
  • Status: applications are not yet open. Final dates and administrative requirements are being completed.

Who can apply

  • You are an early-career researcher based at a university, hospital, research institute or other recognised health or research institution in Africa.
  • You are the presenting author of an abstract, poster or oral presentation already accepted by a recognised scientific meeting.
  • Your work relates to human health, medicine, public health, health systems or a closely related field.
  • You have a genuine funding gap and can show what funding you already have, if any.
  • You can provide proof of your institutional affiliation and conference acceptance.

The final call will define “early career” clearly before applications open.

What the grant can support

  • Conference or congress registration.
  • Economy travel to and from the meeting.
  • Reasonable accommodation required for attendance.

The award can cover all or part of eligible costs up to €1,000. It is intended to fill a funding gap; applicants may combine it with institutional or other legitimate funding. Visa, insurance, local transport and other costs will be addressed explicitly in the final terms before applications open.

What applicants will submit

  • Basic contact and institutional information.
  • A short CV or professional profile.
  • Proof of abstract/presentation acceptance from the conference organiser.
  • The abstract and a plain-language explanation of the research.
  • A short answer explaining what the research could contribute to health or communities and what attending the meeting would make possible.
  • A simple itemised budget, including other confirmed or pending funding.
  • Conference name, dates, location and official website.

How applications will be assessed

Applications will be scored using the same criteria for every eligible applicant:

  • Relevance and potential contribution — 30%: importance of the health question and potential value to patients, communities, research or health systems.
  • Opportunity and career value — 25%: what presenting at the meeting could enable for the applicant's development, network or research.
  • Financial need — 25%: whether the grant addresses a genuine, clearly documented funding gap.
  • Clarity and feasibility — 20%: clear application, legitimate conference, realistic budget and practical plan to attend.

The selection is not a judgement of which disease, country or applicant is “most deserving”. The purpose is to make one small, transparent contribution where it can meaningfully improve access to scientific participation.

Verification and payment

Before an award is confirmed, MHH will verify the conference, acceptance documentation and institutional affiliation. Where practical, eligible costs may be paid directly to the conference, airline or accommodation provider rather than as an unrestricted cash transfer. Receipts and confirmation of attendance may be required.

MHH will never ask applicants to pay an application fee or send money in order to receive the grant.

Independence, conflicts and recipient stories

The grant will not require recipients to promote MHH, provide favourable coverage or alter their research. Any MHH article, interview or social-media story about a recipient will require separate consent and will not influence selection.

Conflicts of interest will be disclosed and managed. A person with a close personal or professional relationship to an applicant should not be the sole decision-maker on that application.

Privacy

MHH will request only information needed to assess and administer the pilot. A short privacy notice covering what applicant information is collected, how long it is retained and who can access it will be published before the application form opens. Sensitive documents should not be sent until that process is in place.

Timeline

Applications are not yet open. The application window, decision date, eligible conference period and application form will be published here once the pilot's administrative and payment arrangements have been finalised.

Important note

This pilot is being developed as an independent MHH initiative. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or funded by Nomi's employer. Final terms may change before applications open.
