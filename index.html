NEW · MHH PILOT

Helping African researchers get their work into the room.

MHH is developing a small Researcher Access Grant for an Africa-based early-career health researcher whose work has been accepted for presentation at a recognised scientific meeting. The pilot will provide up to €1,000 toward eligible registration, travel or accommodation costs.

Applications are not yet open. Full eligibility, dates and application details are being finalised.

WHY MHH?

Health knowledge shouldn't stop at borders, or at jargon.

Research lives in journals. Courses sit on separate platforms. Patient knowledge lives somewhere else. MHH connects those pieces and adds context — with African and diaspora communities especially in mind.
