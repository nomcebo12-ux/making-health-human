PREVENTION & RISK

What can lower risk?

Prevention should never become blame. Type 2 diabetes risk is shaped by genetics, age, environment, access to healthy food, opportunities to move, socioeconomic conditions and other factors — alongside behaviours that people may be able to change.

WHO · PREVENTION
