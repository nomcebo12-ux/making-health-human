How MHH chooses resources

We look for credible authorship, clear purpose, useful access and relevance to a defined audience. We prefer original sources and deliberately look beyond a single country or health system.

A note about health information

MHH is educational and does not replace medical advice from your healthcare team.

Editorial independence

MHH is an independent personal project. Its founder works in communications at the European Association for the Study of Diabetes (EASD). EASD resources are included only where they meet the same selection criteria used for other organisations. MHH does not speak for or represent EASD.
